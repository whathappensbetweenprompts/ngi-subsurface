Subpart RR Monitoring Atlas

Every carbon storage site that reports to EPA under Subpart RR files a plan saying how it will watch for leaks. This atlas reads all 53 approved plans and records what each one commits to measure. The instruments are mostly there. The commitment to share what they record is not.

About this atlas

Each plan was coded against the same guide: 17 monitoring methods, each marked committed, conditional (only if something triggers it), mentioned only, or not found. Every entry cites the plan page it comes from, and most carry a short quote. The 23 Permian plans were read by readers in session; the other 30 were coded through Anthropic's API. Each set was checked by a separate agent that re-coded four sites blind: 92.6% and 97.1% agreement on commitment level. ADM's Illinois plan was hand-coded because its PDF text layer is unreadable.

Operating status is separate from the plans. Twenty sites appear in EPA's public Subpart RR data as having reported stored CO2, but that data stops at 2023 because EPA has not released later years. For the other 33, status comes from state injection reports and well registers in Texas and Michigan, and elsewhere from state permit records, operator announcements and trade press, read on 16–17 October 2026 and checked by separate agents. Where the only evidence is a site's own plan describing wells already injecting, the card says so. Oil recovery units count as storing while their CO2 floods run, because some injected CO2 stays underground; one that now injects only recycled CO2 does not count.
